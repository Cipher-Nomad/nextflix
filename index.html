Upload your Netflix viewing history. You'll get two suggestions based on it. Keep the one you prefer and it stays on screen against a new challenger. After a few rounds you'll have your pick.

  1. On a computer or phone browser (not the Netflix app), open netflix.com/viewingactivity and sign in.
  2. If asked, choose your profile. You can also get there from Account → Profiles → your profile → Viewing activity.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the list and click Download all.
  4. You'll get a file called NetflixViewingHistory.csv. Upload it below.

This file lists titles and dates only, so it can't tell how much of something you watched. For shows, Nextflix treats two or more episodes as "watched" and a single episode as "tried". You can fix any title on the next screen.

  1. The account owner opens netflix.com/account/getmyinfo in a browser and submits the request.
  2. Confirm it from the email Netflix sends you.
  3. Wait for the "your data is ready" email. Netflix says it can take up to 30 days, but it often arrives within a day or two. The download link expires after a couple of weeks.
  4. Download and unzip the file. Open the CONTENT_INTERACTION folder and upload ViewingActivity.csv below.

This file records how long each session lasted, so Nextflix can separate what you finished from what you dropped. It also covers every profile on the account, and you'll choose yours on the next screen. Trailers and previews are ignored.

Your free Gemini key

Suggestions come from Google's Gemini, using your own free quota. You only need to do this once.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with any Google account.
  2. Click Create API key. If asked, let it create a new project.
  3. Copy the key and paste it here.
Connected ·

Your key stays in this browser and is sent only to Google. The free tier has a daily limit per key, and Google may use free-tier requests to improve its products.
